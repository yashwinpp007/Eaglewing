STEP TWO

FREE SITE AUDIT

Before we write a single line of your service plan, one of our senior operations managers visits your facility in person. We walk every zone, identify risk areas, assess current hygiene standards, and understand the specific needs of your people.

This audit is completely free. It's how we make sure the plan we build is built for your facility — not a facility that looks like yours on paper.

  • On-site walk with senior operations manager
  • Zone-by-zone risk and needs assessment
  • Current hygiene benchmarking
  • Written audit report provided at no cost

03
STEP THREE

YOUR CUSTOM PLAN

Using the findings from your site audit, we build a comprehensive service plan that covers every detail: cleaning scope and specifications, DynamiClean™ scheduling, team allocation, product selections, QR zone setup, and Command™ dashboard configuration.

You review and approve the plan before we begin. No surprises. No hidden costs. Clear deliverables and measurable standards from day one.

  • Full scope of works document
  • DynamiClean™ adaptive schedule
  • Command™ platform configuration
  • Fixed, transparent pricing
STEP 03 — CUSTOM PLAN VISUAL
04
STEP FOUR

ONGOING DELIVERY

This is where most cleaning companies stop thinking critically — and where EagleWing doubles down. Our teams follow the plan exactly. Every zone is scanned via QR on arrival and departure. Every task is logged. Every service generates a CleanProof™ report delivered automatically to you.

Your dedicated account manager conducts monthly performance reviews. If standards slip, we know before you do — and we fix it.

  • QR scan check-in on every zone, every visit
  • CleanProof™ report after every service
  • Monthly performance review with your account manager
  • Continuous DynamiClean™ schedule optimisation
