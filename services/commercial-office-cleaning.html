COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How often should an office be cleaned?

+
Most offices benefit from daily cleaning of high-touch areas and common spaces, with thorough cleans 3–5 times per week depending on occupancy. EagleWing's DynamiClean™ adapts frequency to your actual usage patterns — so you're never paying for a clean that isn't needed.

Do you clean outside business hours?

+
Yes. Most of our office cleaning is performed after hours — typically between 6pm and 10pm — to avoid disrupting your team. We can also arrange early morning, weekend, or public holiday cleaning where required.

Are your cleaning staff background checked?

+
All EagleWing team members undergo a formal National Police Check before placement and are rechecked annually. Every staff member is also formally trained in our service standards and holds appropriate certifications.

What is CleanProof™ reporting?

+
CleanProof™ is EagleWing's verified reporting system. After every service, you receive a timestamped report delivered automatically to your email — showing every task completed, every zone serviced, and your cumulative facility audit score.

Do you require lock-in contracts?

+
EagleWing does not require lock-in contracts. We operate on a rolling monthly agreement — you keep us because we're delivering exceptional results, not because you're contractually obligated.
