COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How quickly can you mobilise?

+
EagleWing can mobilise within 24–48 hours for most event cleaning requests. For urgent same-day requirements, call us directly on 0413 391 353 and we will do our best to accommodate your timeline.

Do you clean during the event as well?

+
Yes. We offer discreet during-event cleaning crews who maintain bathrooms, clear rubbish, and service common areas throughout your event without disrupting guests or drawing attention.

Can you do end-of-lease cleans?

+
Yes. EagleWing provides comprehensive end-of-lease cleaning for commercial properties — covering all areas required for bond return or property handover compliance, with documented evidence included.

Do you clean after builders?

+
Yes. Post-construction and builders cleans are a specialty — removing dust, debris, adhesive residue, and construction waste to transform a worksite into a move-in ready, presentation-standard facility.

Is there a minimum job size?

+
There is a minimum engagement fee for one-off cleans. Contact us for a quote and we can advise based on your specific job — most commercial one-off cleaning requests meet our minimum without issue.
