COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can you clean while our facility is operating?

+
Yes. We develop site-specific safety management plans that allow cleaning to occur safely alongside active manufacturing or warehouse operations. All staff are inducted to your site's procedures before commencing.

What industrial floor cleaning do you offer?

+
We offer concrete floor scrubbing with industrial ride-on and walk-behind auto-scrubbers, high-pressure washing up to 3000 PSI, heavy-duty degreasing treatments, and anti-slip coating application for compliant floors.

Are your staff trained for industrial environments?

+
All industrial cleaning staff hold current WHS induction certifications and receive site-specific induction training before commencing. PPE requirements are strictly enforced at all times on industrial sites.

Do you handle chemical spills?

+
EagleWing provides hazardous material cleanup support for minor spills within our licensed scope. For major incidents requiring specialist hazmat response, we coordinate with licensed contractors on your behalf.

Can you do a one-off industrial deep clean?

+
Absolutely. We offer one-off industrial deep cleans for facility changeovers, pre-audit cleans, or periodic deep clean programs — in addition to ongoing maintenance cleaning contracts.
