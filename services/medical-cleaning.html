OUR APPROACH

HOW WE CLEAN HEALTHCARE FACILITIES

01

Clinical Risk Zone Assessment

We map your facility by infection risk level — from low-risk admin areas to high-risk clinical zones — and assign appropriate protocols and products to each.

02

Infection Control Plan Developed

A detailed written cleaning specification is created to AS/NZS 4187 and ACHS standards, with product selections, dilution rates, and dwell times specified for every zone.

03

Colour-Coded Microfibre Protocol

Strict colour-coded microfibre systems prevent cross-contamination between clinical areas, patient rooms, amenities, and common areas.

04

Terminal & Interim Cleans Executed

Full terminal cleans between patient occupancy and interim spot cleans during operating hours — delivered with minimal disruption to clinical operations.

05

CleanProof™ Compliance Reporting

Every service is documented with timestamped records. ATP surface testing available on request for accreditation and compliance evidence.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What standards does EagleWing's medical cleaning meet?

+
Our protocols align with ACHS, AS/NZS 4187, and NSQHS standards. We maintain full documentation for accreditation audits and can provide compliance evidence on request.

What disinfectants do you use?

+
We use TGA-registered disinfectants matched to each zone's risk level — from alcohol-based sanitisers for surfaces to hospital-grade sporicidal agents for high-risk clinical areas like procedure rooms and treatment areas.

Do your staff have healthcare cleaning training?

+
All staff deployed in medical facilities complete specialist infection control training covering healthcare cleaning science, correct PPE use, hand hygiene, colour-coded microfibre protocols, and clinical waste handling procedures.

Can you clean during operating hours?

+
Yes. We schedule spot cleans, terminal cleans between patients, and interim cleaning during operating hours — with full compliance to your infection control policy and minimal disruption to clinical operations.

Do you handle clinical waste?

+
EagleWing provides clinical waste segregation support and works alongside your approved clinical waste contractor. We follow all relevant state health department and EPA guidelines for safe handling and containment.
