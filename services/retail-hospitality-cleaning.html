COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can you clean after trading hours?

+
Yes. Most retail and hospitality cleaning is performed after close of business. We can also arrange pre-opening cleans to ensure your venue is presentation-ready before your first customer of the day arrives.

Do you clean commercial kitchens?

+
Yes. EagleWing provides deep commercial kitchen cleaning including exhaust hood degreasing, cooking equipment surfaces, commercial kitchen floor degreasing, and amenity cleaning — all compliant with food safety standards.

Can you manage multiple store locations?

+
Absolutely. EagleWing manages multi-site retail accounts with a single account manager overseeing consistent standards across all locations — whether you have 2 sites or 20.

What floor care services do you offer?

+
We provide tile and grout cleaning, vinyl stripping and sealing, carpet steam cleaning, polished concrete maintenance, and timber floor care — for all retail and hospitality floor types.

Do you offer window cleaning?

+
Yes. Shopfront and internal window cleaning is available as part of your regular service schedule or as a standalone service. We use streak-free, water-fed pole techniques for impeccable, lasting results.
