COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are your staff cleared to work in schools?

+
Yes. All EagleWing staff deployed in schools hold a current Working with Children Check (WWCC) and National Police Check in addition to our standard vetting requirements. Records are maintained and rechecked annually.

Do you clean during school hours?

+
Typically no — most school cleaning is performed after hours or on weekends. We can however arrange midday spot cleaning of amenities and high-traffic common areas during school days where required.

What cleaning products do you use?

+
We exclusively use child-safe, low-VOC, biodegradable cleaning formulations — GECA certified and safe for use around children. High-touch surfaces receive hospital-approved disinfectants safe for educational environments.

Can you manage a large multi-building campus?

+
Absolutely. EagleWing manages school campuses of all sizes — from single-building primary schools to multi-campus universities. We scale our team and adapt our DynamiClean™ schedule to match your facility's complexity.

Do you provide holiday deep cleans?

+
School holiday periods are ideal for deep cleans, carpet steam cleaning, floor stripping and sealing, and high-level dusting. We plan these into your annual schedule automatically so nothing gets overlooked.
