COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What specialised cleaning services do you offer?

+
EagleWing's specialised cleaning services include carpet steam cleaning, high-pressure washing, window and façade cleaning, floor stripping and sealing, biohazard and trauma cleaning, graffiti removal, solar panel cleaning, and roof and gutter cleaning.

How often should commercial carpets be professionally cleaned?

+
Commercial carpets in high-traffic areas should be steam cleaned every 6–12 months alongside regular vacuuming maintenance. EagleWing can recommend a schedule based on your facility's usage, carpet type, and appearance standards.

Do you do high-rise window cleaning?

+
Yes. EagleWing offers rope access and elevated work platform (EWP) window cleaning for multi-storey commercial buildings. All operatives hold current working at heights certifications and we carry all required insurances.

What is biohazard cleaning?

+
Biohazard cleaning involves the safe removal and remediation of biological contaminants including blood, bodily fluids, and hazardous waste. EagleWing's certified biohazard team responds discreetly, professionally, and in full compliance with relevant health regulations.

Can you quote for a one-off specialised clean?

+
Yes. All specialised cleaning services are available as one-off engagements or as scheduled additions to your regular cleaning contract. Contact us for a tailored quote based on your specific requirements.
