COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What areas does strata cleaning cover?

+
EagleWing's strata cleaning covers all common areas including lobbies, foyers, lifts, stairwells, corridors, car parks, bin rooms, pool surrounds, gym areas, and all building entrances and exits.

Do you provide reports for owner corporations?

+
Yes. CleanProof™ reports are formatted to meet owner-corporation and strata manager documentation requirements, making AGM and audit reporting straightforward. Full service histories can be exported at any time.

How often should common areas be cleaned?

+
Frequency depends on the building's size and occupancy. Most residential complexes benefit from weekly or twice-weekly cleaning of lobbies and lifts, with fortnightly car park and monthly bin room attention.

Can you work around residents?

+
Yes. We schedule strata cleaning during morning hours when most residents have left for work, and time lift and lobby cleans around peak usage periods to minimise inconvenience.

Do you handle graffiti removal?

+
Yes. EagleWing provides graffiti removal services for strata and commercial properties as part of our specialised cleaning offering — available as a reactive callout or scheduled inspection service.
