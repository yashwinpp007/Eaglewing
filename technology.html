Smart Systems & Sensor Integration

EagleWing Command™ integrates with IoT sensors, occupancy counters, and facility management systems to create a live map of your building's cleaning needs — updated in real time.

Instead of cleaning on a fixed schedule, our smart systems detect when cleaning is actually needed. High-traffic bathrooms get serviced more often. Rarely used meeting rooms less so. The result: better hygiene outcomes and less waste.

  • IoT sensor integration
  • Occupancy-based trigger cleaning
  • Live facility status map
  • Anomaly detection and alerts

ADAPTIVE METHODOLOGY

DynamiClean™ Scheduling

Traditional cleaning schedules are arbitrary — the same frequency regardless of whether your office had 10 people or 200. DynamiClean™ changes that.

By analysing real occupancy patterns, event calendars, and seasonal trends, DynamiClean™ generates optimised cleaning schedules that adapt to your facility's actual rhythm — saving you money without compromising hygiene.

  • Occupancy-adaptive frequency
  • Event calendar integration
  • Seasonal adjustment algorithms
  • Cost-efficiency reporting

VERIFIED REPORTING

CleanProof™ Reporting

Every clean is documented, timestamped, and delivered to your inbox. CleanProof™ reports include before/after status, tasks completed, any issues flagged, and your facility's cumulative audit score.

No more wondering if last night's clean actually happened. With CleanProof™, you have irrefutable proof — and the data to hold us accountable if standards slip.

  • Timestamped task completion records
  • Issue flagging and resolution tracking
  • Cumulative hygiene score trending
  • Exportable PDF and CSV reports

FIELD ACCOUNTABILITY

QR Audit Trail

Every cleaning station, bathroom, kitchen, and high-touch area in your facility is tagged with a unique QR code. Our team members scan on arrival and departure — creating an irrefutable time-stamped record of every service.

You can access the full audit trail through your client portal at any time. Every scan. Every zone. Every visit.

  • Zone-level QR check-in / check-out
  • GPS-verified timestamps
  • Real-time completion notifications
  • Permanent accessible audit history
